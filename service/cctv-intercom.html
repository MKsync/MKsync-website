CCTV Cameras & IP Intercom for Companies

MKsync supplies, installs and maintains CCTV and access systems for offices, factories and buildings in Cairo, using Hikvision and Dahua equipment. We start with a site survey, design the camera layout, install and configure everything, then hand over with training.

Who it's for: For offices, factories, warehouses and residential buildings in Cairo that need reliable surveillance and a team that supports it.
