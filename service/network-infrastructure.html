Our Services

Network Infrastructure & Structured Cabling

MKsync designs and installs complete company networks: passive infrastructure (structured cabling, CAT6, fiber optics, racks and patch panels) and active equipment (switches, routers, access points and firewalls). We test and document every point, then support the network after handover.

Who it's for: For new offices, relocations, factories and any company whose network is slow, messy or undocumented. We cover Cairo and Giza.
