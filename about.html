Who We Are

More Than An IT Supplier. Your Technology Partner.

MKsync Solutions is an IT System Integrator focused on helping businesses build, secure and operate reliable technology environments.

We provide practical technology solutions across IT infrastructure, managed IT services, cybersecurity, cloud, Microsoft 365, networking, ERP, business automation and communication systems.

Our approach is engineering-driven and business-focused. We don't believe in selling technology simply for the sake of technology.

We first understand the business, its infrastructure, users and objectives. Then we design the right solution, implement it properly and remain available to support the environment after deployment.
